Sound and voice

Sounds help when nobody is looking at the screen.

2 changes for Whisky 750 ml. They apply from the next part.
Everyone

Look: an instrument, not an office app

  • Keep the dark console colours already in the design tokens; add one cyan "instrument" colour for HUD lines, focus and hover. It is never used for OK or NG.
  • Corner brackets light up on the control under the pointer, like a camera locking on. Panels get thin 1 px frames, no soft shadows.
  • Rajdhani for titles, buttons and the OK/NG word (made in India, has Devanagari). Noto Sans for text in all 15 languages. A mono face for every number so digits never jump.
  • Live image shows a scan sweep, the camera's real telemetry (exposure, gain, ms) and the tool boxes locking on in green or red.
Everyone

Motion with a purpose

  • Each part gets one short moment: the result block pops and the frame turns green or red. Under 300 ms, nothing else moves.
  • The Running dot pulses slowly so the operator sees from across the line that it is alive.
  • Alarms flash at 2 Hz at most, as the spec asks. "Reduce motion" turns all of this off.
  • Hover is a bonus only. Every action also works by touch and keyboard, with no hover needed.
Everyone

Sound design

  • Each sound means one thing, and the operator learns it in a day: a low double buzz is a bad part, a rising sweep is Start, a falling sweep is Stop, a two-tone chime is an alarm.
  • Good parts are silent by default. A beep 225 times a minute stops meaning anything.
  • Hover ticks are very quiet and only for mouse users. Volume and each sound can be set by the supervisor.
  • Alarms can be spoken in the operator's language. Sounds are made in the app, so no audio files to ship or translate.
Operator

Run screen for someone who rarely uses a computer

  • One giant answer first: OK or NG with its shape, readable from several metres.
  • Every warning has one button, "What do I do?", which opens numbered steps with pictures, read aloud, and each step ticks off.
  • Only safe actions on screen: Start, Stop, Change product, Start batch, Parts, Help. Stop asks once.
  • Tap a bad part to see it large, with the fault circled and one sentence saying what is wrong.
Supervisor

Settings in plain words

  • Questions instead of parameters: "How strict should the check be?", not "Threshold 0.62".
  • Every change is tested at once on the product's saved good and bad pictures, and the effect is shown before saving: "198 of 200 good parts still pass, 18 of 18 bad parts caught".
  • Choices show a small drawing of what will happen on the line.
  • Changes collect in one save bar with Undo all; nothing applies by accident. Numbers like ms and thresholds stay in the engineer workspace.
Engineer

Workspace keeps its density

  • Same look and sounds, but the dense VisionPro-style layout stays: job tree, picture, properties, results.
  • Hover on a tool box shows its live value and margin. Hover on a chart mark shows the part.
  • Keyboard first: every action keeps its shortcut and tooltip.
How we build it

Build in four steps, each its own pull request

  1. 1 · Look and feel baseTokens for the instrument colour and fonts, the bracket hover and pressed states, the sound service with its sounds and settings, and Reduce motion. Applied to every existing button and panel.
  2. 2 · Operator Run screenResult pop, live image scan and telemetry, pulsing status, NG strip, and "What do I do?" with steps, pictures and voice.
  3. 3 · Supervisor settingsPlain-word settings with the live test on saved pictures, picture choices, and the save bar with Undo all.
  4. 4 · Engineer polish and checksHover values in the workspace, and the 1366 × 768 layout tests and snapshots for every screen in all three themes.
